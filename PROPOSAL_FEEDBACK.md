# Project Proposal Feedback

The project idea is interesting and relevant to BU students, and comparing library records against survey responses is a good way to see where the two sources differ. However, the proposal needs much more detail. The goal of predicting what books a student will check out is not yet measurable, so please define the prediction task (for example, predicting a book's subject category or genre rather than a specific title), the metrics you will use (such as top-k accuracy or precision at k), and a baseline to beat, like always recommending the most popular category. The data plan depends on BU Library Services sharing data, so please describe a fallback if they decline, and address privacy and ethics, including how data will be anonymized and whether you need IRB or library approval before surveying students or using checkout records. For the survey, say how many responses you expect, how you will recruit students, and how you will avoid a sample biased toward certain majors or years, since small or skewed data could make predictions unreliable. Please also name the features you will use (year, major, and anything else), the models you plan to try, and how you will split data for training and testing. The timeline is too vague, since it only says it will take "a few weeks" to collect data, so add week-by-week tasks, including cleaning, modeling, evaluation, visualization, and the final report. Finally, proofread for small typos (such as "dew weeks").
