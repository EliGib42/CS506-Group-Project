Our project aims to collect BU Library reading data to accurately predict what books (both for classes and for personal reading) an individual student is likely to check out from the BU library based on their year and major.
We plan on asking the BU Library services if they are able to share any of their existing data on this for our project to start, with the hope that they might provide a good base for our data sample.
We will also be getting more data of our own from students through surveys and polls which will rely more on self reporting for what books students check out. We may also compare our survey data to the library data to see where they differ.
Our goal is to write a program that can successfully predict what type of books any given student is mostly likely to check out based on the data pool we have available.
As for our timeline, it will likely take a dew weeks to collect all of the necessary data, as we will need to talk with BU library services as well as make and run our own polls to get more data of our own.
Once we have enough we will then spend the rest of the time writing our code to make the predictions
